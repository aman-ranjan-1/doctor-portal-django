Healthy Habits That Make a Difference

Simple lifestyle changes can help improve your physical and mental well-being while reducing the risk of many common diseases.

  • Drink plenty of water every day.
  • Eat a balanced and nutritious diet.
  • Exercise regularly.
  • Sleep at least 7–8 hours daily.
  • Schedule routine medical checkups.
